🚇 METRO TRANSIT Yellow Line • Rapid Metro

Metro Travel

Use the Yellow Line to travel seamlessly between Delhi and Gurugram during rush hour to avoid heavy traffic jams. Switch at Sikanderpur for Rapid Metro into Cyber City.

Rahul S., Daily Commuter
☀️ WEATHER & TIMING Golden Hour

Best Time to Visit

Early mornings or late evenings are ideal for visiting open-air historical monuments like Qutub Minar. Beat the midday heat and capture golden hour photography.
